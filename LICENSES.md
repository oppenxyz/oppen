building, modification for personal use, and trading as described in that
license, while reserving redistribution, hosted-service and resale rights.
Public source access does not remove those restrictions. GitHub's root-license
badge does not describe the separate desktop terms. These summaries do not amend
the linked licenses or grant additional rights.

## Legal review still required

The custom desktop license and CLA have not been reviewed by project counsel.
Before a stable release, the owner should have counsel review their compatibility
with public GitHub hosting and forking, the intended commercial restrictions,
contribution grants, third-party notices, and trademark wording. This publication
and documentation update is not that legal review. The existing license texts
remain unchanged; any substantive licensing change needs an owner decision.
