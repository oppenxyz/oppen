01 — Why oppen

Trading is about to be rebuilt by agents. The keys should stay with you.

We chose oppen because of Oppenheimer. We believe DEXs are going to bring a huge impact to the financial industry in the following years and can transform retail trading just like the atomic bomb transformed humanity. oppen is local-first: the wallet, the strategy and the execution engine run on the trader's own machine, and the interface is built for agents to operate as much as for people to watch. The brand carries that without theatrics: instrument housings, character matrices, and a contrast floor low enough that the only bright thing on screen is the number that just moved.

Three principles
Everything is an instrument. Panels are housings, not cards. Corners are bracketed, edges are ticked. An agent should be able to read the screen as easily as a person.
Contrast is earned. The interface sits in the bottom 15%. Brightness marks a fact, never a mood.
Own the weight. The reference is a detonation. Never soften it into cuteness.

The reversed E

A standard E mirrored on its vertical axis — not the Latin letter Ǝ. Mirror the glyph so the wordmark stays searchable and reads as "oppen" to a screen reader.

Clearspace & minimums

Clearspace equals the cap height of the O on all sides. Minimum width 96px on screen, 24mm in print. Below that, the mark alone.

When the E is yellow

Only when the wordmark is the brightest element in the frame — covers, OG cards, print. Inside the app it is mono, because the accent belongs to live data.

08 — Voice
Write
"Three books. One fill."
"Runs on your machine."
"Agents trade. You hold the keys."
"Routed to Lighter. 14ms."

Short declaratives. Venue names, latencies and mechanics stated outright. "Agent" is a plain noun, never a persona. Full stops, not exclamation marks.

Don't write
"Unleash your trading superpowers"
"We're nuking the competition 💥"
"Meet Oppie, your AI trading buddy"
"Revolutionising DeFi, together"

No hype verbs, no emoji, no mascots for the agent, no safety claims we can't audit, and never a bomb joke. The reference is gravity, not a punchline.
